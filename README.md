# 2011 Toyota Camry front wheel bearing DIY guide

An offline-friendly, illustrated home-workshop guide for removing the front steering knuckle, replacing its press-fit wheel bearing with the identified OEMTOOLS 57342 loaner, and reassembling and checking the front corner.

**Target application:** U.S.-market 2011 Toyota Camry base, 2.5L, non-hybrid. The repair side, VIN/build configuration, actual bearing part, and replacement hardware have not been recorded.

> **Important safety gate:** This is an independent DIY project, not an OEM Toyota manual or a certification of the repair. The exact-year Toyota jack and safety-stand support-location figure remains an unresolved prerequisite in the guide. Obtain and match it before lifting the vehicle. Verify the actual vehicle build, bearing and hardware fit, torque-wrench usable ranges, and the loaner tool instructions and contacts before starting work. Stop at each unresolved gate.

## Open the guide

Open index.html in a modern browser. The procedural guide and its diagrams work locally without installation or a network connection. Source links need internet access. A [printable PDF](output/pdf/camry-front-bearing-guide.pdf) is also included. Use the page's Print / Save PDF button to generate a fresh copy.

The guide includes a 48-step sequence, assembly-state views, joint-specific torque and socket references, stop conditions, a local job worksheet, a source register, and seven generated illustrations: two exploded part overviews and five bearing-tool bench views. It covers tool fit and load paths as conditional checks; it does not supply guessed adapter sizes, a universal press-screw torque, or a confirmed Toyota fitment for an unidentified replacement bearing.

## Project choices and assumptions

The workflow is designed around ordinary hand tools already available to the DIY mechanic, plus two borrowed specialty tools: the OEMTOOLS 57342 wheel-bearing/hub tool and a high-range torque wrench rated up to 250 ft·lb. It does not assume an air impact, hydraulic shop press, or outsourced bearing pressing. See [PROJECT-SCOPE.md](PROJECT-SCOPE.md) and [PARTS-AND-TOOLS.md](PARTS-AND-TOOLS.md) for what is known, assumed, and still unverified.

No repair is represented as completed. Exact part numbers for the bearing and related replacement hardware, and the torque wrench model, have not been confirmed.

## Privacy and reuse

The repository contains no vehicle registration number, full VIN, address, or owner-specific identifier. The worksheet saves entries in the current browser only and does not upload them. Do not enter a full VIN, registration plate, name, address, or other personal information.
