## Tool plan

### Expected hand-tool kit

The website describes a typical metric set with suitable sockets and wrenches, ratchets, extensions, breaker bar, pliers, cutters, hammer, punch/staking tool, soft mallet, trim pick, cleaning supplies, wheel chocks, and rated lifting/support equipment. Socket sizes in the guide are reference starting points; physically verify every socket on the actual fastener and do not assume previous replacement hardware matches.

### Specialty and measurement equipment

- OEMTOOLS 57342 loaner: manual-screw hub/bearing tool. No hydraulic press or outsourced bearing pressing is assumed.
- 250 ft·lb maximum torque-wrench loaner: the rating covers the guide's highest listed axle-nut value only if the wrench is appropriate and in calibration. Check low and mid-range settings separately; the 250 ft·lb maximum alone does not establish their accuracy.
- Small and/or mid-range torque wrench: needed if the high-range loaner does not accurately cover the lower settings.
- Rated floor jack, stands, saddles, and wheel chocks: the exact support contact locations remain a required Toyota-document check.
- Caliper hanger, axle/knuckle support, and boot-safe tie-rod separator: use suitable rated equipment and the maker's instructions.
- Dial indicator with a rigid mount: required to complete the guide's hub play/runout acceptance checks. A micrometer is useful for rotor thickness checks.
- Snap-ring pliers and a measuring caliper: useful for controlled bench work and adapter/contact checks.

Tool ownership beyond the two identified loaners and the general hand-tool assumption has not been verified.
