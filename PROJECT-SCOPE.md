# Project scope and assumptions

## Target

This guide is prepared for a U.S.-market 2011 Toyota Camry base model with the 2.5L non-hybrid powertrain. It does not cover a hybrid, another model year, or an assumed vehicle build. The front repair side is not recorded. The actual vehicle and its build-specific service information must be checked before parts are ordered or the vehicle is lifted.

## Intended DIY route

The planned route uses common hand tools at home, removes the steering knuckle, and performs hub extraction, bearing removal, bearing installation, and hub installation with the identified OEMTOOLS 57342 hand-screw tool loaner. The assembly is then reinstalled and inspected.

The plan avoids buying or owning a hydraulic press and does not rely on a machine shop for the bearing press work. It also does not depend on an air impact. The tool's actual loaner instructions and adapter fit must be confirmed on the physical kit before force is applied.

## Assumptions and constraints

- Common metric sockets, ratchets, extensions, breaker bar, pliers, hammer, punch, and basic cleaning tools are assumed available. The exact set and brands are not recorded.
- The bearing/hub hand-screw kit is identified as OEMTOOLS 57342, AutoZone SKU 347191. The tool is a loaner. Its adapters, condition, and case instructions must be inspected directly.
- A loaner torque wrench is described as having a 250 ft·lb maximum. Its maker, model, calibration status, and usable low-end range are unknown. A maximum rating does not prove it can accurately cover the smaller torque values in this repair.
- No part number or manufacturer for the replacement bearing, axle nut, snap ring, dust deflector, or cotter pins is recorded.
- A reusable hub is conditional on inspection and applicable measurements. Replace it if damaged or outside the applicable specification.
- A dial indicator and rigid fixture are needed for the guide's measured hub checks. Access to them has not been confirmed. Alignment and some ABS/VSC checks may require service equipment.

## Required gates before physical work

1. Obtain the matching Toyota vehicle lifting and support-location service figure. The owner's floor-jack illustration does not establish safety-stand locations. Do not lift until stand contact points and saddle fit are verified.
2. Confirm the actual vehicle build, repair side, bearing and replacement hardware fit using an authoritative catalog or service source. Do not place a full VIN or plate into this repository or its worksheet.
3. Read the instructions supplied with the actual OEMTOOLS 57342 kit. The retrieved OEMTOOLS 27213 manual is only a reference for general tool arrangements; equivalence and adapter-letter mapping to 57342 have not been established.
4. Trial-fit each adapter by hand and verify support surfaces, clearance, alignment, tool condition, and the complete movement path before loading the screw.
5. Confirm each torque wrench's calibrated, usable range covers the specific fastener setting. Borrow an additional range if needed.
6. Resolve required hub measurements, brake checks, alignment, and applicable ABS/VSC procedures before declaring the repair complete or driving.

## Publication boundary

This is a procedural learning project, not a record of a completed repair or a Toyota-issued procedure. It preserves explicit source distinctions: exact-year reprints, adjacent-year corroboration, installer demonstrations, manufacturer general principles, and unresolved checks. The original schematics show relationships rather than measured Camry geometry. Generated images explain selected tool concepts and do not establish dimensions, part fit, or safe physical setup.
